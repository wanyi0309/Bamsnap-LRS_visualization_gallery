Bamsnap-LRS · visualization gallery

Long-read alignment visualization comparison

Explore matched genomic regions across Bamsnap-LRS, Wally, and SVhawkeye. The gallery includes insertion, deletion, inversion, duplication, RNA-splicing, and phased-SNP highlighting examples and preserves the visualization output available for each tool.

How to use. Select a visualization class and locus to keep all available tools synchronized. Click an image to open the viewer; use the mouse wheel or +/− to zoom and drag to pan. Double-click toggles between fit-to-window and 100%. Use ←/→ to move between loci. Deep links use ?type=INS&id=INS1, so individual examples can be cited or shared. SV and RNA examples compare all three tools; phasing examples compare Bamsnap-LRS and Wally. A “Not available” card means no corresponding image was present in the uploaded result set.
